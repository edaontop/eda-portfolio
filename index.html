About Me

Hello!

I'm Eda, a sixteen-year-old management and development enthusiast from Prague, Czech Republic. Despite my age, I have several years of hands-on experience managing projects, businesses, and networks of various sizes. I focus on creating efficient systems that allow teams and projects to operate smoothly, grow properly, and stay reliable over the long term.

My main goal is to expand my experience and create a welcoming environment where people can perform at their best and where projects can grow consistently. I have been interested in management for more than four years, starting in early 2022, when I first began working with passionate and dedicated teams and learning how to organize projects and work with new people. In 2025, I also started focusing on web and Discord bot development. While I am still expanding my technical experience, I enjoy learning new things and gradually building my skills in development.

Outside of work, I spend most of my time with my family, training in the gym, and spending time in nature. I am also a passionate glider pilot.
